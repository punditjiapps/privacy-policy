Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

Thank you for using Pundit Ji Online (“we,” “us,” or “our”). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your information is handled. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website https://www.punditjionline.com.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, phone number
  • Account Information: Username and authentication credentials
  • Profile Information (Optional): Date of birth, time of birth, place of birth, gender
  • Service Data: Session bookings, consultation history, and user preferences
  • Communication Data: In-app chat messages and audio/video consultation data generated during sessions
  • Technical Data: Device information, crash logs, and performance diagnostics

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and manage consultation services
  • To enable secure one-on-one sessions between users and a professional consultant
  • To manage booking and scheduling functionality
  • To send service-related notifications and reminders
  • To improve application performance and user experience

3. Virtual Credits System

The application uses a virtual credits system to access consultation services. Credits are a non-cash in-app unit used only within the application and have no monetary value outside the app. Payments for credits are processed securely through third-party payment providers.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or share personal data for advertising or tracking purposes. We may share limited data only with trusted service providers (such as payment processors) or when required by law.

5. No Tracking Policy

We do not use personal data for tracking across third-party apps or websites, and we do not use data for advertising profiling.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS and TLS encryption to protect your data during transmission and storage.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide services. Inactive accounts may be removed after a defined retention period.

8. Account & Data Deletion

Users can delete their account directly from the app at any time. Account deletion permanently removes personal data, session history, and related records from our systems.

If needed, users may also request deletion by contacting us at punditjiapps@gmail.com.

9. User Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Request deletion of your account and data

10. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected with a revised “Last updated” date.
